# HISABAI

SPEAK. TRACK. COLLECT.

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

HisabAI is a voice-first digital ledger designed for small vendors
and shopkeepers.

It allows vendors to record business transactions using natural voice
input and manage their customers, sales, received payments, outstanding
udhaar, and payment reminders from a single application.

The system is designed around a simple workflow:

SPEAK → TRACK → COLLECT

## PROBLEM

Small vendors frequently need to manage:

* Customer transactions
* Daily sales
* Payments received
* Outstanding udhaar
* Customer information
* Payment follow-ups

Traditional manual record keeping can require repeated writing,
manual calculations, and remembering customer balances.

HisabAI provides a digital alternative that is centered around
natural voice interaction.

## SOLUTION

HisabAI allows a vendor to speak a transaction instead of manually
entering all the information.

The AI prepares the transaction as a structured ledger entry.

The vendor can then save the transaction and manage the resulting
business information through the application.

## KEY FEATURES

1. VOICE-FIRST TRANSACTION ENTRY

The vendor can use the "Tap to speak" feature to provide a transaction
using natural speech.

2. MULTILINGUAL INTERACTION

The application is designed to support:

* Hindi
* Marathi
* English
* Hinglish

3. MANUAL TRANSACTION ENTRY

Users can also add a transaction manually when they do not want to
use voice input.

4. DIGITAL TRANSACTION LEDGER

Saved transactions contain business information such as:

* Customer
* Items
* Total
* Paid
* Outstanding

5. BUSINESS DASHBOARD

The dashboard provides a clear view of:

* Today's Sales
* Received
* Outstanding
* Customers
* Recent Activity

6. CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT

The Customers section allows vendors to:

* Add customers
* Update customers
* Delete customers
* View outstanding balances
* Manage reminder-related settings

7. PAYMENT REMINDERS

HisabAI includes an AI-powered, consent-aware payment reminder
workflow.

Customers with outstanding payments can be enabled for reminders
after the required consent is provided.

8. DARK / LIGHT MODE

The application provides both Dark and Light interface modes.

## DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The product is built around three simple actions:

SPEAK
Record a transaction naturally.

TRACK
Monitor sales, customers, payments, and outstanding udhaar.

COLLECT
Manage payment reminders for customers with outstanding balances
through a consent-aware workflow.
